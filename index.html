Button not working? Windows, Mac & Linux help

Windows: Press Win + R, paste steam://open/console, then press Enter.

macOS / Linux: Open steam://open/console using a browser or a system URL handler that supports Steam links. If that fails, launch Steam with the -console argument. Availability can vary by installation.

2

Choose a build and copy

Click Copy command next to the version you want.

3

Paste into Steam

Paste the command into the Steam Console and press Enter. Steam will download the files to its depot download folder. Wait for Steam to show the download location.

You may need to own or have access to the app and depot. These are Steam downloads, not direct file downloads from this website.
